Brew Tracker privacy policy

Brew Tracker does not collect personal data.

Your beans, brews, notes and photos are stored on your iPhone and, if you turn on iCloud, in your private iCloud account, which only you can access. There are no analytics, ads or tracking.

Apple Intelligence features run on your device.

If you buy Brew Tracker Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple. We do not see your payment details.

To delete your data, delete the app and remove its data in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
