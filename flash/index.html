The 1-minute way

Flash in your browser

Follow along. Each step shows what you should see.

  1. 1

    Turn the stick on

    Press and hold the small button on the left side for 2 seconds until the screen lights up. A blank or demo screen is fine.

  2. 2

    Plug it into your computer

    Use the USB-C port on the bottom of the stick. Plug straight into the computer if you can, not through a hub.

  3. 3

    Press Install

    A window opens listing ports. Pick the one that looks like the stick, then press Connect:

    Mac
    Windows
    USB Single Serial (cu.usbserial-…)Pick this
    cu.Bluetooth-Incoming-Port
    USB-Enhanced-SERIAL CH9102 (COM3)Pick this
    Communications Port (COM1)

    Then choose Install Brew Tracker Shot Timer, say yes to erasing the stick, and wait. Keep the cable in until it says it's done.

    This browser can't flash. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer, or use the manual way. Flashing needs a secure page. Open https://teenykoh.github.io/flaffee-firmware/flash/ directly.

    No port in the list? Jump to "I don't see a port".

  4. 4

    Watch it fill up

    Writing takes about a minute. The stick restarts by itself at the end.

    Writing… 64%
  5. 5

    Say hello

    The stick shows a coffee cup, then READY and 00:00. That's it. Unplug it whenever you like.

    READY

Last step

Pair with Brew Tracker

  1. Open Brew Tracker on your iPhone and allow Bluetooth when it asks.
  2. Check Use M5 shot timer is on in Settings (it is on unless you turned it off).
  3. Keep the stick close and switched on, and tap Connect shot timer at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Pull a shot. The timer starts by itself when the pump vibrates and sends the time to the app.

From now on, new versions arrive through the app, over Bluetooth. You won't need this page again unless something goes wrong.

The manual way

This uses esptool, the official tool for these chips, typed into the Terminal. Copy and paste each line; you don't need to know any code.

  1. 1

    Download the firmware file

    Save it to your Downloads folder. Don't open it.

    Download shot_timer-2.99.merged.bin
  2. 2

    Get Python

    Most Macs already have it. If the next step says command not found, install it from python.org.

    Install it from python.org. On the first screen of the installer, tick Add python.exe to PATH.

  3. 3

    Open the Terminal

    Press ⌘ Space, type Terminal, press Return.

    Press the Windows key, type PowerShell, press Enter.

  4. 4

    Install esptool

    python3 -m pip install --user esptool
    py -m pip install esptool
  5. 5

    Plug in the stick and flash

    With the stick on and plugged in, paste this. It finds the stick by itself.

    cd ~/Downloads && python3 -m esptool --chip esp32 --baud 921600 write_flash 0x0 shot_timer-2.99.merged.bin
    cd $HOME\Downloads; py -m esptool --chip esp32 --baud 921600 write_flash 0x0 shot_timer-2.99.merged.bin

    You'll see Writing at 0x… lines, then Hash of data verified. Press the side button to restart the stick.

What do you see?

Tap what's happening and we'll tell you what to try.

No port in the list

  1. Swap the cable. A charge-only cable is the cause nine times out of ten.
  2. Make sure the stick is on (hold the side button for 2 seconds).
  3. Plug straight into the computer, not a hub or a monitor.
  4. Windows, or a Mac that still shows nothing: install the USB driver from M5Stack's download page (look for CH9102), restart the computer and try again.

"Failed to connect" or "Timed out"

  1. Close the Arduino IDE, Cura, or anything else that may be using the port.
  2. Unplug the stick, wait 5 seconds, plug it back in and press Install again.
  3. Still failing: hold the side button for 6 seconds to turn the stick fully off, then plug it in. It starts in a state that's easier to talk to.

It stopped part way

  1. Nothing is broken: the stick can always be flashed again.
  2. Unplug, plug back in and press Install again. Choose to erase when asked.
  3. Try a different USB port, or the manual way with --baud 115200 in place of 921600 (slower, steadier).

The screen stays black afterwards

  1. Press the side button once. The stick may simply be asleep.
  2. Charge it for 10 minutes on the cable, then hold the side button for 2 seconds.
  3. Flash again and choose to erase the stick when asked.

The app can't find the stick

  1. Check the stick shows READY.
  2. On the iPhone: Settings, Brew Tracker, and allow Bluetooth.
  3. Keep the stick within a metre or two while pairing.
  4. Force-close Brew Tracker and open it again.

There's no Install button

  1. Flashing in the browser works in Chrome and Edge on a Mac or Windows computer.
  2. Safari, Firefox, phones and iPads can't do it. Use Chrome, or the manual way.

Still stuck? Email support with a photo of the screen and what you tried.
