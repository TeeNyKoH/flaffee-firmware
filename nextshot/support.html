Support

Help, feedback and bug reports.

Next Shot is made by one person, and I read every message. For now, the quickest way to reach me is through TestFlight (see below).

Early access

  • Install TestFlight from the App Store, then open the invite link.
  • Early builds expire after 90 days. TestFlight tells you when a new one is ready.
  • To send feedback, open Next Shot in TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback. Say what you tapped and what you expected, and attach a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

  • My data. It lives on your iPhone. To keep it safe, turn on iCloud sync in Settings, or make a backup from Settings and save it to iCloud Drive.
  • Moving to a new iPhone. With iCloud sync on, sign in to the same iCloud account. Otherwise restore from a backup file in Settings.
  • The coach is locked. The free version includes your first coach suggestions. Next Shot Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks the full coach and unlimited beans. Use Restore Purchases in Settings if you bought it before.
  • Reminders. Each reminder can be turned off in Settings. If none arrive, check Notifications for Next Shot in the iPhone Settings app.

More answers are on the questions section of the home page. How the app handles your data is in the privacy policy.
