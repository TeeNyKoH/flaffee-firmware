Snap the bag

Your real bag, on your phone.

Photograph a bag and its background is removed on your iPhone, so it stands on your shelf like the real thing. Spin it, flip it and zoom in. Keep the roaster, origin, roast date, price and how much is left.

When a bag runs low, a reminder tells you when to order the next one.

Log the shot

A coach for every single shot.

Drag a ruler for grind, dose and yield, or tap the number to type it. Last time's numbers are already there, so you only change what changed.

After the shot, the Next shot card says what to do: finer, coarser or keep it, with the reason in plain words.

Rate it

Rate it. It remembers.

Tell it how the cup tasted: sour, bitter, thin, sweet. The coach remembers the grinds that worked on each bag, and learns your grinder's steps and your taste as you go.

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence it also reads your tasting comments and explains its advice in friendlier words.

See what works

Your habit, at a glance.

Cups this week, your streak, your busiest day. Your flavour profile on a wheel, and every origin on a globe. Search finds any bean, brew or roaster.

Questions

Is it free?

Free to start: up to 5 beans and your first coach suggestions. Next Shot Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks the full coach and unlimited beans. No subscription, and Family Sharing is on.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your iPhone. If you turn it on, it also syncs through your own iCloud, so your other devices get it. Backups go to your iCloud Drive. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Logging, the shelf, your stats and the coach all work without a connection. Syncing and backing up to iCloud need one.

Which grinders and machines does it work with?

Any. You enter your grinder's own scale once (marks, steps per mark, stepless or with clicks), and every shot is logged on those numbers. The coach reads your grinder's own dial.

Do I need a scale or a timer?

No. It works with just your phone. You type in the weights and the time, or drag the rulers.

How does the coach decide?

It looks at your time, your yield, your ratio and how you rated the cup, and remembers the grinds that worked on that bag. After each shot it suggests one change with the reason. With milk, it judges the shot on time and ratio, because milk hides sour and bitter.

Does it need Apple Intelligence?

No. The coach works on every iPhone that runs iOS 26.1 or later. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence it also reads your tasting comments and explains its advice in friendlier words, and that happens on your phone.

Does it work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. Open an iPhone Duo flat and every screen becomes two pages. Fold it shut and it carries on as a normal iPhone app.

Which iPhones can run it?

Any iPhone on iOS 26.1 or later. It is iPhone only.

When is it on the App Store?

Install TestFlight from the App Store, then open the invite link. Early builds expire after 90 days, so update when TestFlight asks.
