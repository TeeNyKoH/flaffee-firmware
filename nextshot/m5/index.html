What it is

A tiny gadget with one job.

The M5 shot timer is an M5StickC Plus 1.1: a small coloured stick with a screen, three buttons, a motion sensor and a microphone. We put Brew Tracker software on it, and it does one thing very well. It knows when a shot starts and when it stops.

  • It feels the pump. Espresso machines shake a little when the pump runs. The stick has a motion sensor that notices.
  • It listens, too. The microphone confirms the shot, and can learn what your own machine sounds like.
  • It tells your iPhone. Over Bluetooth. No Wi-Fi, no account, no cable after the first set-up.
  • It shows the next move. Before you pull, the screen shows the grind to use and the yield to aim for.

Why you would want one

Because shots do not wait for your thumbs.

Pulling a good shot already takes both hands. A timer should not be one more thing to remember.

Two hands, one shot

You cannot start a phone timer, lock in the portafilter and place the cup at once. The stick starts itself.

No lost seconds

A hand timer starts when you remember. The stick counts from the very first moment the pump ran, even though it needs a second and a half to be sure.

No retyping

Without it, you note the time and type it into Log Brew. With it, the draft is waiting with the time filled in.

Pre-infusion, understood

It tells the gentle first stage from the main flow, does not stop early in a pause, and shows "Pre 6 s · Main 22 s" on the brew.

A plan before you pull

The READY screen shows the coach's move (finer, coarser, keep), the grind to use and the yield to aim for.

A better coach

The coach reads real times, pre-infusion and the shot graph, not just numbers typed from memory.

Best for home espresso drinkers who dial in each new bag, and for machines with no built-in shot timer. It is for espresso only: it senses the machine, so it does not time filter brews.

How it works

How it knows a shot has started.

No buttons to press. The stick follows what the machine does.

  1. Vibration starts a shot. When the pump runs, the stick moves to CHECKING.
  2. It checks for 1.5 seconds. Most of the readings (70 per cent) must look like a real shot. Then it times from the very first moment, so nothing is lost.
  3. Pre-infusion is allowed. In the first 15 seconds it waits through 6 seconds of quiet before calling the shot over, so a pause does not end it early.
  4. It stops by itself. When the vibration fades for a second and the machine is quiet, the shot ends at the last moment the pump was running.
  5. Flushes are ignored. Anything under 5 seconds is not logged as a shot.

Built and tuned on a Gaggia Classic. It should work on any espresso machine whose pump vibrates. More machines are being tested during the Alpha.

Beans and your next shot

Choose the bag on the stick. See what to expect.

You do not have to reach for your phone to say which coffee you are pulling. Pick the bag on the stick, and the stick shows what to expect before you start.

The bean picker

  1. Click the bottom button on READY (before the shot) or on DONE (before you log it). The picker opens with the cut-out picture of the bag and its name.
  2. Press the big M5 button to move to the next bag.
  3. Click the bottom button again to confirm the bag. The top button closes the picker with no change.

Every bag you have not marked Finished is in the list, up to 32, and a new bag appears as soon as you save it in the app. The pictures are the real cut-outs of your own bags, sent to the stick from the app.

What the pick does

  • Before the shot: the bag you pick is used for the next shot: its draft, the READY line and the time band. It lasts for one shot, or 2 hours, whichever comes first.
  • After the shot: the app moves the draft to that bean, with that bean's last numbers.
  • The bean you pick on the stick always wins for that shot, in the draft, the shot summary, Log Brew and the Lock Screen.

Sound profile

Teach it what your machine sounds like.

Vibration always starts a shot. Sound helps the stick decide when it is really over. A sound profile makes that decision about your own machine, in your own kitchen.

Why bother

Out of the box, the stick uses fixed loudness levels. They work well on the machine it was built on. A different machine, a noisy grinder next to it or a loud extractor fan can look quite different. The profile replaces the fixed levels with a fingerprint of your setup.

What it changes, and what it does not

  • It changes only the sound part of detection.
  • Vibration still starts every shot and sets the start time. Sound never starts a shot.
  • With the profile on, "sounds like the shot" replaces the fixed loudness tests, and the stop needs half a second of not sounding like the shot. The loudness gate stays as a safety check.
  • It is off by default. No profile, or the switch off: the stick behaves exactly as before.

How you teach it

  1. Quiet room

    5 seconds of the kitchen with the machine idle.

  2. Grinder (optional)

    8 seconds of the grinder running. You can skip this.

  3. A real shot

    Pull a normal shot. It is kept as a normal shot, with a draft, so nothing is wasted.

It checks the result

The shot must last at least 15 seconds, be clearly louder than the quiet room, and sound clearly different from the grinder. You then Save it, or Try again with the reason (shot too short, too close to the quiet room, too close to the grinder).

Shadow mode first

Once saved, the stick quietly records on each shot whether the profile would have agreed, and shows it on the shot summary. Nothing changes yet. After 9 of the last 10 shots agree, the app suggests "Turn it on".

You stay in control

In the shot timer settings: a Use sound profile switch, Redo sound profile and Forget profile (with a confirmation). One profile only. The stick keeps it, and the app keeps a copy to send back if the stick ever loses it.

Updates

Flash once. Update from the app.

You use a computer for the very first set-up only. After that, the app checks for new stick software each time it opens. When one is ready, the timer bar says "Timer update available".

Safe by design

The update goes into a spare slot, is checked, then the stick restarts. If anything fails, the old version keeps running and the app offers to try again.

Never at a bad moment

Not during a shot, and not below 30 per cent battery.

Keeps going in the background

You can close the update screen. Progress shows in the timer bar and on the Lock Screen.

Needs the internet once

Your phone downloads the update, then sends it to the stick over Bluetooth.

What you need, and how to start

From the box to your first shot.

You need: an iPhone on iOS 26.1 or later, the full edition of Next Shot (Alpha testers get the install link by email), an M5StickC PLUS 1.1, a USB-C data cable, and once only, a Mac or Windows computer with Chrome or Edge. Phones and iPads cannot flash the stick.

  1. Flash it, once

    The set-up guide does it from your browser in about a minute. The stick arrives blank, so this puts the Brew Tracker software on it.

  2. Pair it in the app

    Turn on Use M5 shot timer, allow Bluetooth when the app explains why, then tap Connect shot timer. The phone sets the stick's clock for you.

  3. Put it on the machine

    Anywhere it can sense the pump's vibration: on, against or beside the machine. Pull a shot. The stick should go from READY to CHECKING on its own. If it does not, try a spot nearer the pump.

  4. Pull a shot

    Watch it on the stick, the app and the Lock Screen. Tap to log it, and the time is already there.

Straight talk

What it cannot do.

  • iPhone only, iOS 26.1 or later. No Android, no iPad.
  • It connects to the full edition of Next Shot only. The App Store edition has no Bluetooth link.
  • Espresso only. It senses the machine, so it does not time filter brews.
  • Bluetooth only. There is no Wi-Fi link, and the phone sets its clock.
  • Your phone needs to be in Bluetooth range for live updates. Shots made apart are kept (the last 10) and sent when it reconnects.
  • Shots under 5 seconds are ignored.
  • Only the M5StickC PLUS 1.1 is supported.

Questions

Does it work with my machine?

It should work with any espresso machine whose pump vibrates. It was built and tuned on a Gaggia Classic, and more machines are being tested in the Alpha.

Where do I put it?

Anywhere it can sense vibration: on, against or beside the machine. Pull a shot and watch the screen. If it goes from READY to CHECKING by itself, you have found a good spot.

Does it work with the App Store version?

No. It connects to the full edition of Next Shot. The App Store version does not connect to the stick. Alpha testers get the install link by email.

Can I choose which bean I am brewing on the stick?

Yes. Click the bottom button on READY or DONE, press the big M5 button to move through your bags, and click the bottom button again to confirm one. The draft follows your pick.

What does the stick show before I pull?

The bean and its last time, the coach's next move (finer, coarser, keep or prep), the grind to set, and LAST, AIM and EXPECT for the time band.

Do I need to flash it?

Yes, once, from a Mac or Windows computer with Chrome or Edge. It takes about a minute. After that, updates come from the app.

Can I flash it from my phone or iPad?

No. Read the guide on your phone if you like, and flash from the computer.

Does it use Wi-Fi?

No, only Bluetooth. Your phone sets the stick's clock when they connect.

Does it work when my phone is locked?

Yes, while the stick is in Bluetooth range. Shots made out of range are kept (the last 10) and sent when it reconnects.

What if an update fails?

The stick keeps its old version and the app offers Try again.

Will it start for a flush?

No. Anything under 5 seconds is ignored.

What about pre-infusion?

It waits through pauses in the first 15 seconds, and shows the split, for example "Pre 6 s · Main 22 s".

Do I have to use the sound profile?

No. It is off by default and the stick works without it. It is there if your machine or kitchen confuses the fixed levels.

Does it time filter brews?

No. It senses an espresso machine's pump, so it is for espresso only.

Can I turn it off in the app?

Yes. Settings > Shot Timer > Use M5 shot timer. Switched off, the app never uses Bluetooth and asks for no permission.

How long does the battery last?

The app shows the stick's battery and warns you when it is low. Charge it over USB-C.

Where does my data go?

Your shots go from the stick to your iPhone over Bluetooth and nowhere else. For updates, the app downloads the stick software from GitHub. See the privacy policy.

Where do I buy one?

You source it yourself. See "Get one" above for what to search for and what it looks like. We do not sell it or link to a shop.

Is it open source?

No. The software file you flash is public, and the source stays private.
