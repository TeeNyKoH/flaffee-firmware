Privacy policy

Next Shot: Brew Tracker. Last updated November 2026.

Short version: Next Shot keeps your coffee data on your iPhone. There is no account, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. The app does not send your data to the developer or to anyone else.

What the app stores

Everything you enter: beans, bag photos, brews, ratings and tasting notes, your equipment, milks, and your settings. It is stored on your iPhone.

What leaves your iPhone

  • iCloud sync (optional). If you turn it on in Settings, your data syncs through your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. The developer cannot see it.
  • iCloud Drive backups (optional). Backups you make are saved to the Next Shot folder in your iCloud Drive, or wherever you choose to save the file.
  • Sharing and export. If you share an image or export your brews as a file, it goes where you send it.
  • Purchases. Buying Next Shot Pro is handled entirely by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details.

The app makes no other network connections.

Permissions

  • Camera. To photograph a coffee bag. The photo is processed on your iPhone to remove the background, and is never uploaded.
  • Photos (add only). To save images you choose to share, such as a bean card.
  • Notifications. Optional reminders: when to buy a bag again, when a milk carton is nearly out or expires, and to rate a brew. They are scheduled on your iPhone and you can turn each one off in Settings.

Apple Intelligence

On iPhones that have it, the coach can read your tasting comments and explain its advice in friendlier words. This is done by Apple's on-device model, on your iPhone. Your comments are not sent anywhere.

Children

Next Shot collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a bean or brew in the app, or delete the app to remove what is stored on your iPhone. If you used iCloud sync or backups, also remove the Next Shot data in your iCloud settings or delete the backup file in iCloud Drive.

Changes to this policy

If this changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
